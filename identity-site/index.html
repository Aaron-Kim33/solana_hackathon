Lumber Rush

Solana Mobile · Preview

Tap the tree, collect wood, and grow your forest. This page identifies the Lumber Rush app when connecting a compatible mobile wallet.

Wallet login uses a free message signature. In updated builds, a Mainnet commemorative Memo is optional and requires a real SOL network fee; growth rewards never require a record. Older previews used Devnet. No token purchases or airdrop eligibility are offered.
