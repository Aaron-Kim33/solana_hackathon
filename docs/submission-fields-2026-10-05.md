## New mobile development (if not porting an existing app)

Lumber Rush was built as a mobile-first Android game using React Native and Expo, rather
than as a web-app wrapper. During the hackathon, we implemented one-finger hold-to-chop
combat, sweep-to-load wood collection, animated trolley delivery, contextual onboarding,
and server-authoritative progression. Players contribute quest materials to weekly shared
facilities that improve squirrel expedition rewards, participate in cooperative world-boss
goals, view weekly material-contribution and boss-damage rankings, and restore the forest
through a planting and water-path puzzle. Solana Mobile Wallet
Adapter provides free wallet-signature login, while an optional Mainnet Memo records a
growth milestone without blocking free progression. The standalone APK has been tested
on a Seeker device.

This wording relies on the maintainer's reported phone QA, not independent observation.
Do not claim cross-account testing or a demonstrated Mainnet transaction without footage/evidence.
